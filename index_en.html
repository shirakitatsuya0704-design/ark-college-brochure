ARK COLLEGE Philosophy

01. A Paradigm Shift in Education

Old Standard (until 2020)

Knowledge × processing speed

In Japan’s high-growth era, securing a uniform, high-quality workforce made one ability optimal: reaching the right answer quickly and accurately, as measured by standardized deviation scores.

New Standard (2020 onward)

Applied knowledge × creativity

In the age of AI and VUCA, an unpredictable world asks how you use knowledge, who you work with, and what you create.

Admissions Reform and the Three Elements of Academic Ability

1. Knowledge & Skills
Foundational literacy
Where Comprehensive Admissions is won
2. Thinking, Judgment & Expression
Logical dialogue
3. Initiative & Collaboration
Working with diverse others

Comprehensive Admissions is no longer a "one-talent" or "easy" route. It has evolved into the most intellectually demanding selection method, and the one best matched to what society needs today.

02. Skills That Last Long After Admissions

— The new model of learning defined by the international framework "Education 2030" —

"Education 2030," the global education framework, calls for the ability to carve out a future with no fixed answers: cultivating "agency" — a sense of ownership. In the age of AI, rote learning has lost its value. What admissions and society reward now is not theory on paper but "the power to move reality".
At Ark College, we build three next-generation skills on this principle.

1. Posing the Question

Problem Posing

When answers are everywhere online, the question itself holds the value. Questioning convention and finding problems from your own perspective is the source of your Statement of Purpose and research.

2. Acting in the Real World

Authentic Action

You learn from real engagement with society, not from borrowed words. Authentic experience — taking on challenges without fear of failure — produces the one-of-a-kind language that moves an interviewer.

3. Orchestration

Orchestration

Creating new value through dialogue with diverse others rather than working alone. This skill makes the decisive difference in group discussions and in project work after you enroll.

Preparing for Comprehensive Admissions is training that earns you a lifelong advantage.

Admission is a milestone. Together, let us build the strength to keep shining long after it.
